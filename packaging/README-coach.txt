HOCKEY STATS
============

What it does
------------
Reads the InStat PDFs you get after each game (the Match report and the
Player report) and shows a full analysis of the team, lines, defence pairs,
passing and players in your web browser. Everything stays on your Mac.

First time you open it
----------------------
The app isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once:

  1. Unzip the download and drag "Hockey Stats" into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click it. macOS says it can't check the app. Click "Done" (or "OK").
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and next to
     "Hockey Stats was blocked..." click "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password.
     (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.)

After that, double-clicking just works.

Every time
----------
  1. Double-click Hockey Stats. Your browser opens the app (there is no window
     or Dock icon; the app lives in the browser tab).
  2. Drag the two PDFs for a game onto the page (or click "Choose files...").
     Games are remembered, so next week you only add the new game.
  3. Use the menu on the left: Overview, Lines & pairs, Pair chemistry,
     Players, and so on. "How to read this" explains every number.
  4. "Save report (HTML)" makes one file you can email; it opens in any browser.
  5. "Quit app" closes it (it also closes by itself 15 minutes after you close
     the browser tab).

Where your games are kept
-------------------------
  ~/Library/Application Support/HockeyStats
Delete that folder to start fresh.
