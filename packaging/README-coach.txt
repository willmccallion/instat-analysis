HOCKEY STATS
============

What it does
------------
Reads the InStat PDFs you get after each game (the Match report and the
Players' report) and shows a full analysis of the team, lines, defence pairs,
passing and players in your web browser. Everything stays on your Mac.

Installing
----------
  1. Open Hockey-Stats-mac.dmg. In the window that appears, drag Hockey Stats
     onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Hockey Stats.
  3. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from the
     App Store). Click "Done", then open Apple menu > System Settings >
     Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
     "Hockey Stats was blocked". Enter your Mac password.
     (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.)

After that, double-clicking just works.

Every time
----------
  1. Double-click Hockey Stats. Your browser opens the app (there is no window
     or Dock icon; the app lives in the browser tab).
  2. The first time, click your team.
  3. Drag the two PDFs for a game onto the page (or click "Choose files...").
     Games are remembered, so next week you only add the new game.
  4. Use the menu on the left: Summary, Rankings, Lines & pairs, Players,
     Team & goalies, and so on. "How to read this" explains every number.
  5. "Save report (HTML)" makes one file you can email; it opens in any browser.
  6. Closing the browser tab closes the app (or use "Quit app").

Updates
-------
When a new version is out, the app shows an "Update now" button at the top of
the page. Click it and the app updates and reopens by itself. (Updates need the
app to be in your Applications folder.)

Where your games are kept
-------------------------
  ~/Library/Application Support/HockeyStats
Delete that folder to start fresh.
