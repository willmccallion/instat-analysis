Hockey Stats

Drop in your InStat game reports and see which lines and defence pairs work, who's playing well, and what to work on in practice.

Set it up on Linux (about 1 minute)

  1. Run the install command

    Open a terminal, paste the command above and press Enter. It puts the app in ~/.local/bin and adds Hockey Stats to your applications menu. No password needed.

  2. Open it

    Choose Hockey Stats from your applications menu (or run ~/.local/bin/hockey-stats).

  3. Pick your team and add a game

    Your web browser opens Hockey Stats. Click your team, then drag in the two InStat PDFs for a game (the Match report and the Players' report). Games are remembered, so next time you only add the new one.

    • To remove it later, run the same command with sh -s -- --uninstall in place of sh. Your saved games stay in ~/.local/share/hockey-stats until you delete that folder.

Set it up on Windows (about 2 minutes)

  1. Keep it somewhere handy

    Open your Downloads folder and move Hockey-Stats-windows to your Desktop or Documents. That one file is the whole app. Keep it in a folder of your own (not Program Files) so it can update itself.

    • If your browser warns that the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep (in Edge: … › Keep › Keep anyway).

  2. Open it first time only

    Double-click Hockey-Stats-windows. Windows will say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't from the Microsoft Store. That's expected:

    • Click More info.
    • Click Run anyway.

  3. Pick your team and add a game

    Your web browser opens Hockey Stats. Click your team, then drag in the two InStat PDFs for a game (the Match report and the Players' report). Games are remembered, so next time you only add the new one.

Set it up on a Mac (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open the download

    Click Hockey-Stats-mac.dmg in your browser's downloads (or in your Downloads folder). A window opens showing Hockey Stats and your Applications folder.

  2. Drag Hockey Stats onto Applications

    That installs it. You can then close the window and eject "Hockey Stats" from the sidebar of any Finder window.

  3. Open it first time only

    Open your Applications folder and double-click Hockey Stats. Your Mac will say it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store. That's expected:

    • Click Done.
    • Open the Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security.
    • Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to "Hockey Stats was blocked", then enter your Mac password.
    • On older Macs: right-click Hockey Stats, choose Open, then Open again.

  4. Pick your team and add a game

    Your web browser opens Hockey Stats. Click your team, then drag in the two InStat PDFs for a game (the Match report and the Players' report). Games are remembered, so next time you only add the new one.

Good to know

Nothing else to installEverything the app needs is included. It opens in the browser you already use.
Updates itselfWhen there's a new version, the app shows an Update now button. One click and you're done.
Your data stays on your computerReports are read on your computer and never uploaded anywhere.
Closing itClose the browser tab and the app closes too. Double-click it to come back.
